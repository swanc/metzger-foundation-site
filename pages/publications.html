Publications

Interviews with Hans Ulrich Obrist

Gustav Metzger: Interviews with Hans Ulrich Obrist

Hauser & Wirth Publishers

A richly illustrated volume drawing from more than two decades of conversations with Hans Ulrich Obrist, artistic director of the Serpentine. Covers Metzger's childhood, the 1966 Destruction in Art Symposium, and his visionary thinking about environmental destruction. Complemented with a rigorously researched chronology.

Writings (1953–2016)

Gustav Metzger: Writings (1953–2016)

Edited by Mathieu Copeland · JRP|Editions, 2019 · 696 pp.

Bringing together more than 350 texts written between 1953 and 2016, this comprehensive volume establishes Metzger as one of the towering figures of the twentieth century. Includes seminal writings such as the manifestos of auto-destructive and auto-creative art, essays about architecture, and an interview with R. Buckminster Fuller from 1970.

Act or Perish!

Act or Perish! — A Retrospective

Nero, 2016

A comprehensive retrospective publication surveying Metzger's career from the late 1940s onwards, published on the occasion of the exhibition at CoCA Toruń, Poland.

Null Object

Null Object: Gustav Metzger Thinks About Nothing

Black Dog Publishing, 2012

An exploration of Metzger's philosophical and artistic engagement with the concept of nothingness, void, and the immaterial in his work.

Historic Photographs

Gustav Metzger: Historic Photographs

New Museum, 2011

Published on the occasion of the first US solo museum exhibition of Metzger at the New Museum, New York, highlighting his engagement with historical trauma and representation.

Gustav Metzger (2009)

Gustav Metzger

Verlag Der Buchhandlung Walther König, 2009

Published on the occasion of Decades 1959–2009 at the Serpentine Gallery, London.

Geschichte Geschichte

Gustav Metzger: Geschichte Geschichte / History History

Edited by Sabine Breitwieser · Generali Foundation / Hatje Cantz, 2005 · 310 pp.

The first comprehensive retrospective catalogue in Austria, with texts by Justin Hoffmann, Gustav Metzger, Kristine Stiles, and Andrew Wilson.

Damaged Nature

Damaged Nature, Auto-Destructive Art

Coracle @ workfortheeyetodo, 1996 · 110 pp.

Contains Metzger's essay ‘Nature Demised Resurrects as Environment’ (1992), the ‘Auto-Destructive Art’ manifesto, Andrew Wilson's essay, and an extended chronology and bibliography by Clive Phillpot.

Manifeste, Schriften, Konzepte

Manifeste, Schriften, Konzepte

Edited by Justin Hoffmann · Verlag Silke Schreiber, 1997

A collection of Metzger's manifestos, writings, and conceptual texts, published in German.
