The Foundation

The Gustav Metzger Foundation is a UK-based charity that was founded upon Metzger's death in 2017. During his lifetime, Metzger defined the organisation's mission by envisioning not only exhibitions of his work and furtherance of the political and philosophical ideas he espoused, but also through support for individuals working in the fields of the arts and environmental studies.

Charitable Purpose

The Foundation's charitable purpose, as defined by Metzger during his lifetime, is:

  • To organise exhibitions of his work and convey the political and philosophical ideas which he espoused.
  • To support individuals working in the fields of the arts, environmental studies, or other areas relevant to his ideas and work.
  • “To combat the risk of global extinction arising from the activities of humans.”

Objectives

The Foundation's objectives include:

  • To advance the education, public understanding, and appreciation of the artistic and literary work of Gustav Metzger.
  • To promote national and international conflict resolution and reconciliation for the public benefit, with a view to relieving suffering, poverty, and distress, and building and maintaining social cohesion and trust within and between communities.
  • The promotion of racial and religious harmony for the benefit of the public.

Directors

The Gustav Metzger Foundation is co-directed by Ula Dajerling and Leanne Dmyterko.

Gallery Representation

In 2020, Iwan Wirth, Manuela Wirth, and Marc Payot announced Hauser & Wirth's representation of the Estate and Foundation of Gustav Metzger. The first exhibition of his work at the gallery was at Hauser & Wirth Somerset from 26 June to 12 September 2021.

The Gustav Metzger Foundation Scholarship

Hauser & Wirth partnered with Dartington Arts School by supporting a student on the ‘Arts and Place’ MA course. The Gustav Metzger Foundation Scholarship is eligible to a student with refugee status or who has an application for asylum in the UK, covering tuition fees, living expenses, and a residency at Hauser & Wirth Somerset.
