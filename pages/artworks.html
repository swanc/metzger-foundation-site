Artworks

Acid Action Painting

Acid Action Painting

1960/2004

Nylon, hydrochloric acid, metal. Height 7 ft, Length 12' 6", Depth 6 ft. Three nylon canvases coloured white, black, and red arranged behind each other. Acid is painted, flung, and sprayed onto the nylon which corrodes at point of contact within 15 seconds.

Liquid Crystal Environment

Liquid Crystal Environment

1965/2005

Five projectors with heat-sensitive liquid crystals create an immersive environment of constantly changing colour projections. Originally made in 1965, remade in 2005. The work represents a pivotal shift from destruction to creation in Metzger's practice.

Earth from Space

Earth from Space

1966

Metzger's first mechanised sculpture with liquid crystals. A work that merged scientific experimentation with artistic expression, reflecting his ongoing interest in the intersection of art and technology.

Dancing Tubes

Dancing Tubes

1968

A kinetic work that uses air currents to animate flexible tubes, creating an ever-changing sculptural form. The work embodies Metzger's interest in chance, movement, and the interplay between the controlled and the uncontrollable.

Mobbile

Mobbile

1970/2017

A modified car with a transparent cube affixed to its roof, into which a pipe feeds exhaust fumes from the vehicle's engine. The work collects and stores its own carbon emissions — a prescient statement on pollution and environmental destruction. Re-presented in Glasgow during COP26 in 2021.

Earth Minus Environment

Earth Minus Environment

1992

A model addressing car pollution and environmental destruction. Part of Metzger's sustained engagement with ecological themes and his vision for art as a vehicle for environmental awareness.

Historic Photographs

Historic Photographs

1995–ongoing

A series of works engaging with historical trauma and representation, exploring the relationship between photography, memory, and historical violence. The subject of a major exhibition at the New Museum, New York, in 2011–2012.

Flailing Trees

Flailing Trees

2009

Fifteen upturned willow trees embedded in a block of concrete, symbolising a world turned upside down by global warming. Exhibited at the Serpentine Gallery, London, as part of Decades 1959–2009.

Light Drawings

Light Drawings

2014

A series of works exploring light as a medium for drawing and mark-making, representing some of Metzger's final artistic investigations.
