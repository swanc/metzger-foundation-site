Ula Dajerling

Co-Director & Curator

Ula Dajerling has worked closely with Metzger on the realisation of his ambitious ideas and projects and helped produce and curate his artworks nationally and internationally. As part of her work with Gustav Metzger and his estate, she co-ordinated the artist archive and exhibitions, worked on publications, managed and delivered production of Metzger's artworks and collaborated with major global art institutions, including Tate, Sprengel Museum, Hannover, MUSAC, Leon, Martin Gropius Bau, Berlin.

Among other projects, she co-curated the Gustav Metzger exhibition Ethics into Aesthetics at West, Den Haag, NL and worked as a researcher for various institutions, incl. Jumex Museum in Mexico City and Centre of Contemporary Art in Torun, Poland.

Leanne Dmyterko

Co-Director & Curator

Leanne Dmyterko is a London-based curator and Co-Director of the Gustav Metzger Foundation. For nearly a decade, she worked closely with Gustav Metzger on projects and exhibitions internationally, and now continues this work through the Foundation.

She has written extensively on Metzger's life and work for a range of publications and has collaborated on numerous exhibitions of Metzger's work, including co-curating several major solo exhibitions, including presentations at Ben Uri Gallery in London, West in Den Haag, and Kabuso in Bergen, Norway.
